What we take care of

One team for your whole event.

It doesn’t start with the venue but with your brand: what does it stand for, who do you want to reach and what should people remember? From that we build a concept and deliver it from start to finish. You always talk directly to Malte and Julian.

  • Idea & concept

  • Partners & sponsors
  • Food, DJ & programme
  • On-site content

Example

The Berlin Night Run.

Our first own event: around 8 kilometres through Berlin at night, across Kreuzberg and Neukölln. Start and afterparty at Ciao Ciao Bar, three stations at Myxa, Redrum Art Bar and Dietrich Bar, with a DJ on a cargo bike up front. Red Bull and HEJ Natural were part of it, and every partner had a role that matched their brand. Conceived and delivered in a few weeks.

Where we work

We are based in Potsdam and Innsbruck. Berlin is right on our doorstep, and in Tyrol we are currently planning SNOWSIDE, a creator ski week. For the right project we will travel anywhere.

Quick questions

What you might still want to know.

How much does an event with you cost?

There is no fixed price list, because every event is different. After a short call you get a concrete quote. If you already know your budget, feel free to tell us right away.

How much lead time does an event need?

That depends on size, venue and permits. We put the Berlin Night Run together in a few weeks. The earlier you get in touch, the more room there is.

Do you also produce the content for the event?

Yes. Photos, reels and videos from the event are part of the package, so the night doesn’t end after a few hours but keeps going on your channels.
