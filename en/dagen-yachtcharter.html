Project 02 · Corfu · 2026

Dagen Yachtcharter

One week on board, camera in hand: we told the story of sailing in a way a younger audience wants to watch.

Client
Dagen Yachtcharter
When
September 2026
Where
Corfu, Greece
Content
10 reels · daily stories
Our role
Concept, filming, editing, posting

The brief

Anyone can book sailing.
You just have to want to.

Dagen Yachtcharter rents out sailing boats. Their Instagram channel was just getting started. The goal: make chartering appealing to a younger audience and show that a sailing trip is exactly the experience they are looking for.

The execution

One week on board, content every day.

Instead of an ad, we showed what a week on the boat really feels like. We filmed on board, posted stories live every day and made ten reels for the channel.

  • Filming on board

One week around Corfu.
