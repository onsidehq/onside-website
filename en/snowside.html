In planning · Tyrol · March 2027

SNOWSIDE

Our creator ski week: one week in Tyrol, one chalet, selected creators and content that looks like adventure and doesn’t sound like advertising.

When
March 2027
Where
Tyrol
Who
8–10 selected creators
Base
1 chalet · 5 nights · 4 ski days
Status
In planning · looking for partners

The idea

Skiing for the next generation.

Young people don’t want a week in the same ski resort. They want variety, adventure and nights people talk about for a long time. That is exactly what we are building: a week with creators from action, travel and lifestyle instead of the ski bubble. Their communities follow them for adventure, not for kilometres of piste. That is where we meet them.

Why it works

Three sides, one goal.

  • The creators bring reach and credibility with 18 to 30-year-olds who don’t have skiing on their radar yet.
  • The brands appear in moments the creators experience anyway – not in an ad break.
  • We turn it into a week people talk about: concept, organisation, filming and a shared aftermovie.
