Our story

We met studying sports management. But what connected us wasn’t the lecture hall, it was everything in between: festivals, concerts, games, trips. Experiences you still remember years later. At some point, being part of those moments wasn’t enough – we wanted to build them ourselves. That became onside.hq.

What makes us different

We are Gen Z – the very audience other agencies hold workshops about. We don’t need to research what works right now; we see it in our own feed every day. We combine that perspective with strategic thinking and clean execution. The goal isn’t a campaign people scroll past, but something people talk about. And we never start with ourselves, we start with you: every project begins with understanding your brand, your audience and your goals. Only then do we build an experience that carries exactly that.

Why onside?

The name comes from sport. Onside is the opposite of offside. That is exactly our job: bringing brands back from offside into the game – to where attention happens.
