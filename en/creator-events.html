Services · Creator events

Creator events where content happens by itself.

A few days, a special place and the right creators: that’s how you get content that doesn’t look like advertising. We plan these events and content trips and build your brand in so that it naturally belongs.

How it works

We take care of everything around it.

We find creators who fit your brand and your audience, plan the location, schedule and programme and agree in advance what content should come out of it. On site, we are there and keep all the threads together.

  • Creator selection
  • Location, accommodation & schedule
  • Programme & experiences
  • Partners & brand integration
  • Content plan
  • On-site support

For brands

Your brand in the middle of it, not on the sidelines.

Quick questions

What you might still want to know.

How do I apply for SNOWSIDE as a creator?

Applications go through Instagram at @onside.hq. All the details are on the SNOWSIDE page.

Can we become a partner as a brand?

Yes. Drop us a line about who you are and what you have in mind, and we will work out together how your brand fits the event.

Do you also organise creator events just for our brand?

Yes. Alongside our own formats like SNOWSIDE, we also plan creator events and content trips exclusively for one brand.
