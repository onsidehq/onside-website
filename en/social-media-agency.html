What we do

From the idea to the finished reel.

Before we film, we work out what your brand stands for and who you want to reach. That becomes a plan for which stories we tell and on which channel. Then we produce, edit and publish.

  • Reels & short videos
  • Photos & visual style
  • Videos & editing
  • Stories & live content
  • Content calendar
  • Channel management & community

Example

Over 270,000 views with ten reels.

For Dagen Yachtcharter we spent a week on board around Corfu and produced ten reels and daily stories, plus a film for YouTube. Before, the channel’s reels averaged around 200 views, ours around 27,000, roughly 130 times as many. The strongest reel is at 178,000 views.

Content for events

A night that keeps going on your channels.

When we organise an event, we plan the content from the start: which moments do we want to show, where do the cameras go, what goes out the same night? That way one event becomes material for weeks.

Quick questions

What you might still want to know.

Can you manage our channels long term?

Yes, we can take over ongoing management with a content calendar, production and publishing. Often we start with a single project, though, so you can see how we work.

Which platforms do you produce for?

Mainly Instagram, plus longer videos for YouTube. We adapt format and editing to each platform.

How much does social media with you cost?

That depends on the scope, meaning how much content and how much management you need. After a short call you get a concrete quote.
